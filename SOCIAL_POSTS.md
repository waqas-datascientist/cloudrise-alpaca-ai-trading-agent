## Post 1 — Problem and architecture

Most trading agents let one black box both invent and authorize a trade. I'm building CloudRise with a separation of powers: four research agents can propose, but only a deterministic risk governor can approve. The executor has no override. Building for the Alpaca AI Trading Agents Hackathon with @AlpacaHQ and @lablabai. #BuildInPublic #AITrading

## Post 2 — Options design

CloudRise now turns a directional thesis into one defined-risk vertical option spread: 7–21 DTE, liquid quotes, known max loss, atomic MLeg execution. No naked exposure. The most valuable “AI decision” is often refusing an attractive setup that cannot be sized safely. @AlpacaHQ @lablabai #OptionsTrading #FinTech

## Post 3 — A setback worth sharing

Today's lesson building CloudRise: a strong signal is not an executable trade. Wide option quotes can erase the modeled edge, so I moved liquidity into the hard authorization layer and price entries at long ask minus short bid. If the real quote fails, the agent logs “no trade.” @AlpacaHQ @lablabai #BuildInPublic

## Post 4 — Auditability

Autonomy without evidence is just a claim. CloudRise writes every specialist vote, veto, risk gate, Alpaca CLI reconciliation, and broker response to an append-only decision ledger. The dashboard shows why capital moved—or why it stayed still. @AlpacaHQ @lablabai #ExplainableAI #AlgoTrading
